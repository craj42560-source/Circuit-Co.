About Circuit&Co

We are Birmingham's independent tech repair specialists — fixing the devices other shops give up on.

What we stand for

Honesty first

If your device is not worth repairing, we will tell you. No upselling, no hidden fees.

Right to repair

We believe you own your devices and have the right to fix them — anywhere, by anyone.

Sustainability

Every repair keeps a device out of landfill. We also recycle what cannot be saved.

Quality work

Certified technicians, genuine-grade parts, and a 12-month warranty on everything.

Our Mission

To make quality tech repair accessible, affordable, and honest — so that no one throws away a device that still has years of life left in it.

Our Vision

A city where every device gets a second chance, where repair is the default and replacement is the last resort.
